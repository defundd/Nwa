The Archive

"Every garment is a document. We just keep the archive."

Nowear Archive exists at the intersection of fashion history and the present moment. We source pieces that defined eras — runway relics, designer one-offs, culturally significant garments that deserve to be worn, not warehoused.

Our curation spans the decades that shaped how we dress today. Each piece is authenticated, researched, and handled with the respect it has earned.

This is not fast fashion. This is not trend chasing. This is the archive.

I

Curation

Every piece is selected with intention. If it doesn't belong in the archive, it doesn't enter the archive.

II

Provenance

We research what we sell. The story behind a piece is part of its value.

III

Access

The archive is available to wear, to rent, to study. Fashion history shouldn't sit in a box.

IV

Discretion

We operate quietly. The pieces speak. We don't need to.
